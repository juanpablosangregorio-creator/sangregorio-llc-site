Before you buy: what every customer sees at checkout

All our products are digital content with immediate delivery. Immediately after the payment is confirmed the customer receives an email with a secure download link; nothing is shipped physically. Discounts, crossed-out prices and limited-time offers are promotions: the price paid is always the one displayed at checkout when the order is confirmed, in the currency shown.

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee counted from the purchase date: the customer writes to us from the email used at checkout, quoting the order number, and the refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. We also refund duplicate charges and billing errors without further conditions. Full details: Refund Policy · Terms & Conditions · Privacy Policy.
