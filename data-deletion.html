How to delete your data

If you interacted with any of our Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts (such as Mentes Curiosas, @mentescuriosas.kids) or bought one of our products, you can ask us to delete all data related to you:

  1. Email info@juanpablosangregorio.pro with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your Facebook or Instagram username, or the email you used to buy.
  3. We delete your messages, comments and personal data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also remove our app's access at any time: on Facebook, go to Settings → Business Integrations; on Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
